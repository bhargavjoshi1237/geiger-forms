# Pricing Teardown + Undercut Strategy

How each competitor charges, where it hurts the buyer, and exactly how Geiger
Forms prices under it. **Mid-2026; 🟡 indicative unless ✅ — verify before use.**

---

## 1. The two pricing models to beat

### Model A — WordPress annual license (Gravity, WPForms, Fluent, Formidable)
Charge once a year, gate features by license tier and site count.

| Product | Entry | Mid | Top | What's gated up top |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Gravity Forms** ✅ | $59/1 site | $159/3 sites | $259/unlimited | Payments@$159; surveys/quiz/e-sign/registration/webhooks/Salesforce@$259 |
| WPForms 🟡 | ~$50/yr | ~$100–200 | ~$300/yr | Payments/surveys/registration up the ladder |
| Fluent Forms 🟡 | ~$60/yr | ~$110 | ~$220/yr | **More bundled lower** — the value play |
| Formidable 🟡 | ~$100/yr | ~$200 | ~$400/yr | Views/apps/calculators up top |

**Weakness:** WP-only; feature laddering; the *real* stack (workflow + views +
PDF) is extra third-party subscriptions → true cost $600–900+/yr.

### Model B — SaaS subscription with usage meters (Typeform, Jotform, Formstack, Paperform, Tally, Fillout, Cognito, Zoho)
Charge monthly/annually per seat, **meter submissions/responses/forms/storage**.

| Product | Free tier | Entry paid | Mid | Meter that bites |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Typeform** 🟡 | ~10 resp/mo | ~$25/mo | ~$50–83/mo | **responses/mo** |
| **Jotform** 🟡 | 5 forms/100 subs | ~$34/mo | ~$39–99/mo | submissions, forms, storage, views, payments, signs |
| **Formstack** 🟡 | trial only | ~$99/mo | ~$299/mo | ✅ + hidden implementation/overage costs |
| **Paperform** 🟡 | trial | ~$29/mo | ~$59–129/mo | submissions/mo |
| **Tally** 🟡 | **unlimited** | ~$29/mo | — | branding/domain/team, not volume |
| **Fillout** 🟡 | ~1k subs/mo | ~$15/mo | ~$42/mo | submissions/mo |
| **Cognito** 🟡 | ~500 entries/mo | ~$19/mo | ~$39–99/mo | entries/mo |
| **Zoho Forms** 🟡 | limited | ~$10/mo | ~$25–90/mo | submissions, HIPAA up top |
| Google/MS Forms | free | (bundled) | — | none (but no payments/logic) |

**Weakness:** the meter punishes success. A form that works = an upgrade prompt
or overage bill. Payments, HIPAA, e-sign, remove-branding, custom domain, and
seats are all upsell levers.

## 2. Where the money hurts (per-buyer pain points to exploit)

1. **"To take one payment I must jump a whole tier."** Gravity ($59→$159),
   Typeform/Tally-free lacks it, most gate it. ✅
2. **"My form succeeded and now I owe overage / must upgrade."** Every metered
   SaaS. The submission meter is the most hated line item.
3. **"I need e-sign / HIPAA / approvals so now it's the $259 or Enterprise
   tier."** Advanced modules concentrated at the top.
4. **"I'm paying 3 vendors"** — form builder + GravityView + GravityFlow, or
   Typeform + Calendly + DocuSign. Bundling collapses this.
5. **"Hidden onboarding/implementation fees."** ✅ Formstack specifically.

## 3. Geiger Forms recommended pricing

**Principle:** attack *both* models at once — lead with Tally's "unlimited
submissions" generosity on the low end, and bundle Gravity-Elite/Formstack-grade
capability into a single flat plan on the paid end. **No per-submission tax.**
Undercut on *what's included*, not just sticker.

### Proposed tiers (illustrative — tune to suite economics)

| Tier | Price | Who | What's included |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Free** | $0 | Individuals, trials | **Unlimited forms & submissions**, all field types, conditional logic, calculations, public link + embed, 1 payment gateway (Stripe), basic analytics, Geiger branding. *(Beats Jotform/Typeform free outright; matches Tally; adds payments like Tally.)* |
| **Pro** | **~$19–29/mo** (or suite-bundled) | Businesses, creators | Everything: **payments (no cap)**, **surveys/quiz**, **e-sign**, **multi-page + save/resume**, **AI form gen + AI response analysis**, remove branding, custom domain, workflow → Geiger Flow, PDF generation, advanced analytics (drop-off), integrations, unlimited responses. *(This is Gravity Elite $259 + GravityFlow + GravityView, for a fraction — and no meter.)* |
| **Team / Business** | **~$49–79/mo** | Teams | Pro + seats, roles/RBAC, shared folders, real-time collaboration, approvals workflow, priority support. |
| **Compliance / Enterprise** | custom | Healthcare, legal, gov | **HIPAA + BAA**, ePHI fields, SSO, audit trails, data residency, SLA. *(The Zoho/Formstack moat — priced under Formstack.)* |

### Why this wins
- **vs Gravity:** no WordPress required; payments + surveys + e-sign in the base
  paid plan, not laddered across $59/$159/$259; and it's cheaper than their top
  tier *before* you add the third-party stack they need.
- **vs metered SaaS:** "unlimited submissions" removes the single biggest source
  of buyer anxiety and overage revenue — a message they structurally can't match
  without hurting their own P&L.
- **vs everyone:** it's inside the Geiger suite, so a Pro sub also buys into
  Notes/Flow/Docs/Events — bundle economics competitors can't touch.

### The one-line market message
> **"Everything Gravity charges $259 + add-ons for, and everything the SaaS
> builders meter you for — payments, logic, surveys, e-sign, and workflow — in
> one flat plan with unlimited submissions."**
