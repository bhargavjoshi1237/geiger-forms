# Competitor Landscape + Adjacent Categories

Two groups: **direct competitors** (form builders) and **adjacent categories**
worth absorbing so one product covers +10% more of the market. Pricing is
mid-2026, 🟡 indicative unless marked ✅ — verify before quoting.

---

## A. Direct competitors — SaaS form builders

### Typeform 🟡
- **Position:** Beautiful, conversational, one-question-at-a-time. The design
  benchmark. Strong for surveys, quizzes, lead gen where experience matters.
- **Strengths:** UX/aesthetics, logic jumps, "Video" typeforms, quiz scoring,
  recall/personalization, strong integrations, good analytics, embeds.
- **Weak:** Expensive; **response-capped** (free ~10 responses/mo; paid tiers cap
  monthly responses hard); limited on payments/complex data tables; can feel
  slow for long data-entry forms (one-at-a-time isn't ideal for 40-field intake).
- **Pricing (🟡):** Free (tiny); Basic ~$25/mo; Plus ~$50/mo; Business ~$83/mo;
  Enterprise custom — all metered on **responses/mo** and seats.
- **Steal:** the conversational default + polish. Geiger already has a
  conversational-capable renderer path; make "beautiful one-question mode" a
  toggle, not a $259 add-on.

### Jotform 🟡
- **Position:** The kitchen sink. 10,000+ templates, huge widget/integration
  library, form → PDF, tables, apps, approvals, sign, store.
- **Strengths:** Breadth — Jotform **Approvals** (workflow), **Sign** (e-sign),
  **Tables** (Airtable-like), **Apps** (no-code app builder), **Store**,
  **Report Builder**, 100+ integrations, payments with 30+ gateways, HIPAA on
  higher tiers.
- **Weak:** Everything is **metered** (form count, monthly submissions, storage,
  views, payment submissions, signed docs). UI is cluttered/busy. Upgrades
  triggered exactly when you succeed.
- **Pricing (🟡):** Free (5 forms / 100 submissions/mo); Bronze ~$34/mo (25
  forms / 1k subs); Silver ~$39; Gold ~$99; Enterprise custom. HIPAA on Gold+/
  Enterprise. *(These numbers are indicative — the verification pass could not
  confirm them; re-check.)*
- **Steal:** the **breadth-in-one-product** model (approvals + sign + tables +
  PDF). This is the single most important competitor to match on scope.

### Formstack (Forms) 🟡
- **Position:** Enterprise/regulated. Forms + Documents (doc gen) + Sign +
  Workflows as a "Productivity Platform."
- **Strengths:** HIPAA, Salesforce-native, document generation, e-sign,
  approval workflows, strong security/governance.
- **Weak:** **Expensive and metered**; ✅ hidden implementation/onboarding and
  overage costs beyond sticker; entry ~$99/mo, Suite ~$299/mo (🟡). Not for SMBs.
- **Steal:** the regulated/enterprise workflow bundle — but at a fraction of the
  price. Their pricing is the ceiling Geiger prices under.

### Paperform ✅ (positioning verified)
- **Position:** "One form builder that runs your business" — ✅ *"capture data,
  onboard clients, generate leads, take payments, trigger workflows and send
  documents for signing, all from one form builder."*
- **Strengths:** Beautiful editor (document-like), payments, calculations,
  **guided/AI form building**, plus **adjacent standalone products**: ✅
  **Papersign** (e-sign), **Stepper** (AI-native workflow automation), **Agency+**
  (agency client/brand mgmt). This is the exact "absorb adjacent categories"
  blueprint Geiger should copy.
- **Weak:** Metered submissions; smaller integration library than Jotform;
  pricing climbs with volume.
- **Pricing (🟡):** Essentials ~$29/mo; Pro ~$59/mo (Stepper bundled); Agency
  ~$129/mo — metered on submissions.
- **Steal:** the multi-product suite framing — **which Geiger gets for free**
  because Notes/Flow/Docs/Events already exist in the suite.

### Tally 🟡
- **Position:** "Notion of forms." Generous free tier is the whole pitch —
  **unlimited forms & submissions free.**
- **Strengths:** Free unlimited, block-based editor (Notion-like), fast, clean,
  conditional logic, calculations, payments (Stripe) even on free, hidden fields.
- **Weak:** Branding on free; advanced (custom domains, remove branding, team,
  integrations) on Pro; thinner analytics/enterprise story.
- **Pricing (🟡):** Free (unlimited forms/subs); Pro ~$29/mo.
- **Steal:** the **"unlimited submissions" headline** — the anti-metering wedge.
  This is the biggest threat to the SaaS incumbents and the vibe Geiger should
  match on the low end.

### Fillout 🟡
- **Position:** Fast-growing "generous free + powerful logic + AI" challenger.
- **Strengths:** ✅-adjacent claim: **1,000 submissions/mo free** (indicative),
  strong conditional logic, scheduling, payments, DB-backed (write to Airtable/
  Notion/Sheets/Supabase), AI features, good-looking.
- **Weak:** Younger, smaller ecosystem/brand.
- **Pricing (🟡):** Free (1k subs/mo); Pro ~$15/mo; Business ~$42/mo.
- **Steal:** the **"forms as a front-end to your database"** angle — write
  submissions straight into external tables. Geiger's Supabase-native design is
  perfect for this.

### Cognito Forms 🟡
- **Position:** Value-for-money power builder. Best free tier among "serious"
  builders.
- **Strengths:** Repeating sections, powerful calculations, conditional logic,
  payments (low fees), e-sign, save-and-resume, **HIPAA on paid**. Very generous
  free (500 entries/mo).
- **Weak:** UI plain; smaller ecosystem/brand awareness.
- **Pricing (🟡):** Free (1 form-ish? actually generous entries); Pro ~$19/mo;
  Team ~$39/mo; Enterprise ~$99/mo (HIPAA at Team+).
- **Steal:** repeating sections + calculations depth; cheap payments.

### Wufoo 🟡
- **Position:** Legacy SurveyMonkey-owned builder. Simple, dated.
- **Weak:** Aging, metered, little innovation. **A churn source to target** —
  their users want a modern replacement.
- **Pricing (🟡):** Free (5 forms/100 entries); Starter ~$14; Professional ~$29;
  Advanced ~$74.

### Zoho Forms ✅ (compliance verified)
- **Position:** Part of Zoho suite; strong value + compliance.
- **Strengths:** ✅ **HIPAA** via "Mark as ePHI" field (auto AES-256 + audit
  log), SSL/TLS 1.2+, **RBAC**, **90-day audit trails**, **BAA on request**.
  Deep Zoho ecosystem, payments, approvals, offline mobile app, sub-forms.
- **Weak:** Best value only if you live in Zoho; UI utilitarian.
- **Pricing (🟡):** Free; Basic ~$10/mo; Standard ~$25; Pro ~$50; Premium ~$90.
- **Steal:** the **compliance moat** — first-class HIPAA/ePHI handling. Highest-
  leverage differentiator for regulated verticals.

### Google Forms / Microsoft Forms 🟡
- **Position:** Free, bundled with Workspace/365. The default "good enough."
- **Strengths:** Free, zero friction, real-time collab, Sheets/Excel sync,
  ubiquitous.
- **Weak:** No payments, weak logic, no branding/theming depth, no workflow, no
  real analytics, no e-sign. Pure capture.
- **Steal:** their **frictionlessness and collaboration** — but Geiger wins the
  moment a form needs logic, payments, or a workflow. Position as "Google Forms
  you don't outgrow."

### WordPress-world others 🟡
- **WPForms** — Gravity's biggest WP rival; freemium (free "Lite" in wp.org),
  Basic ~$50/yr → Elite ~$300/yr; friendlier UI, similar add-on ladder, payments
  at Pro tier, AI form builder added.
- **Formidable Forms** — power builder: **views, calculators, application/
  directory building, relationships**. The "build apps on WP" angle.
- **Ninja Forms, Fluent Forms** — Fluent Forms especially is a fast, cheaper
  Gravity alternative with generous bundling (many add-ons included lower down
  the ladder — a pricing model Geiger should note).
- **Contact Form 7** — free, developer-y, no UI; the zero-cost floor.

### Others to know (survey/quiz/data-heavy) 🟡
- **SurveyMonkey / Qualtrics / Alchemer** — survey-first, research-grade,
  expensive; overlap on surveys/quiz/NPS.
- **Formsort** — developer/product-team onboarding flows, experimentation/
  A-B on form flows, API-first. Niche but a signal for "forms as product
  onboarding."
- **HeyForm** — open-source Typeform alternative (self-host). Signals demand for
  OSS/ownership.
- **Airtable Forms / Notion Forms / Fillout** — "form is a front-end to a
  database" pattern.
- **Feathery, Formcarry, Basin, Getform** — dev-focused form backends (you bring
  the front-end, they handle submissions/spam/integrations).

## B. Adjacent categories to absorb (the +10% coverage)

Each is a category people currently buy separately. Absorbing them = one product
covers more of the buyer's job and displaces a second subscription.

| Category | Standalone products | Why absorb it | Geiger fit |
|---|---|---|---|
| **E-signature** | DocuSign, Dropbox Sign, PandaDoc, Papersign | Intake forms → contracts is one flow; Gravity gates a *drawn* signature at $259 and it's not a real e-sign | Add a Signature field + a "send for signing" flow; store signed PDF |
| **Scheduling / booking** | Calendly, Cal.com, Acuity | "Book a call" is the #1 thing a lead form leads to | A booking field / date-slot picker + calendar sync; or deep link to a Geiger scheduling sibling |
| **Workflow / approvals** | GravityFlow, Kissflow, Zapier, Make | The form *starts* a process (approval, routing, tasks) | Geiger Flow already exists in the suite — route submissions into Flow issues/approvals |
| **Surveys / NPS / quiz** | SurveyMonkey, Typeform, Qualtrics | Same builder, different render + reporting | Geiger already has scoring; add survey field types + report views |
| **Payments / order forms** | Stripe Checkout, Paperform, order plugins | Monetization; the highest-value forms | Native Stripe; products, quantities, coupons, subscriptions |
| **PDF / document generation** | Formstack Documents, WebMerge, GravityPDF | Submissions → branded PDF (invoices, contracts, certificates) | Map answers → PDF template; attach to notifications |
| **Data tables / no-code apps** | Airtable, Jotform Tables, Baserow | Submissions need to be viewed/managed as a database | Responses screen already exists; add a table/grid + views + filters (Geiger is Supabase-native — natural strength) |

### Bonus adjacent bets (differentiators, not table stakes)
- **AI form generation** ("describe your form → built") — Fillout, Paperform,
  WPForms, Jotform all have it now; becoming table-stakes.
- **AI response analysis** — summarize open-text answers, categorize, sentiment.
- **Analytics/attribution** — funnel drop-off, field-level abandonment, UTM
  capture, A/B testing of form variants (Formsort's niche).
- **Front-end display / directories** — GravityView's whole business; show
  submissions publicly as listings/maps/tables.
