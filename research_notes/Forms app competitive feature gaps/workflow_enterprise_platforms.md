# Workflow / Enterprise Form Platforms — Ops-Depth Feature Inventory (Jotform, Formstack/Intellistack, Cognito Forms, Zoho Forms; brief: 123FormBuilder, Wufoo)

Research date: 2026-10-21. All pricing was observed on vendor pages on 2026-10-21 unless marked otherwise. Tags used below: **[VERIFIED-VENDOR]** = read directly on the vendor's own page; **[3P]** = third-party aggregator/review; **[COMPETITOR]** = written by a rival vendor (biased); **[UNVERIFIED]** = could not confirm against a primary source; **[OLDER]** = dated before 2025.

## Q1. What end-to-end "operation cycles" do these platforms support (intake → approval → document → signature → payment → record), and how deep is each step? (includes the per-product lifecycle inventory)

### Takeaway
Jotform is the only vendor here that covers every ops step natively in one account: Workflows, Sign, Tables, Apps, Boards, offline Mobile Forms, 40+ payment gateways and AI Agents. Its depth is capped by monthly quotas rather than feature gates. Formstack (now Intellistack) goes deepest on documents, e-sign and Salesforce, but approvals, documents and e-sign all start at the $250/mo Suite tier. Cognito is the "data-model" specialist: repeating sections, formula calculations, role/status workflow, Word-template document merge and a client portal, at low prices. Zoho is the "field-ops" specialist: multi-level conditional approvals, offline mobile, kiosk, geofencing, and a very fast 2025–26 release cadence. 123FormBuilder and Wufoo are shallow (123FB has basic approvals and PDFs; Wufoo has neither).

### Inferences
- The full intake → approval → document → signature → payment → record chain is native in **Jotform** (Workflows + Smart PDF + Sign + payments + Tables) and **Formstack Suite** (Forms + Documents + Sign + approvals). Cognito covers it at lower depth: e-sign, Word-merge, workflow and Cognito Pay, but no native mobile/offline app was found. Zoho covers it via sibling Zoho apps (Zoho Sign field, Books, CRM, Creator).
- The real architecture split is "one product that does everything" (Jotform: Tables/Apps/Boards/Workflows/Sign all under one quota model) vs "form + adjacent products" (Formstack Forms/Documents/Sign/Streamline; Zoho Forms + Sign/Books/Creator/CRM). Geiger Forms, being suite-integrated, structurally resembles the Zoho model: it can borrow ops steps from sibling apps (Flow for tasks/approvals, Docs for documents) rather than building them all.
- Cognito's distinctive pattern is that **workflow is modelled on the entry itself** (roles + actions + statuses, per-role field visibility, workflow links that need no login). Zoho's approvals add **per-level field permissions, fallback routing and admin override**. These are the concrete approval semantics competitors expose.
- Draft/Publish staging (Jotform Enterprise 2.0) and Partial Submissions + Field Bottlenecks (Formstack) are ops-quality features that aren't obvious from a basic forms feature list.

### Gaps
- Jotform Tables, Report Builder, PDF Editor and Sign: no detailed spec (formula columns, views, signer ordering, audit trail) was retrieved. Only one-line descriptions plus quotas.
- Jotform's exact native field/widget count was not stated on the features page.
- Cognito's formula syntax (Excel-like vs C#-like expressions), conditional-approver configuration and timed actions were not confirmed from a primary page.
- Whether Cognito has a native mobile or offline app was not found (likely none, but unverified).
- No primary Formstack page lists a field count, template count, offline app, or Documents/Sign standalone pricing. The Forms "1,000 submissions per form" label could not be clarified.
- Zoho's USD prices and Enterprise tier rely on 3P aggregators. The vendor page rendered no prices during this session.

## Q2. Which ops features (approvals, PDF docs, e-sign, tables, mobile offline) are must-haves for business buyers vs. niche?

### Takeaway
Based on where vendors place features in their tiers: **approvals, e-signature, save & resume, payments and PDF output are mid-tier table stakes**. Every workflow-oriented vendor offers them at or below about $25–50/mo, and e-sign often ships on free tiers. **HIPAA/BAA, SSO, audit logs, data residency, and custom document templates are enterprise gates** everywhere, sold as top-tier upsells. **Offline mobile and kiosk** are present only in Jotform and Zoho, making them a field-ops differentiator rather than a universal must-have. **AI agents, phone/voice and MCP/LLM connectors** are 2025–26 differentiators that are fast becoming expected.

### Cited Findings
- **Approvals at mid-tier, even free:** Jotform Workflows on Free with up to 3 approvers — [Jotform Workflows](https://www.jotform.com/products/workflows/). Zoho approvals on Standard+ (~$25–30/mo) — [Zoho Pricing](https://www.zoho.com/forms/pricing.html); [Capterra](https://www.capterra.com/p/227463/Zoho-Forms/) [3P]. Cognito Workflow & Approvals on Team ($39–49/mo annual) — [Cognito Pricing](https://www.cognitoforms.com/pricing). 123FB "Approve Submissions" on Gold ($18.5/mo yearly) — [123FB](https://www.123formbuilder.com/signup.html). Outlier: Formstack gates approval routing to Suite ($250/mo) — [Formstack Pricing](https://www.formstack.com/pricing).
- **E-sign broadly included:** 10 signed docs/mo on Jotform's free plan — [Jotform Pricing](https://www.jotform.com/pricing/). Digital Signature on 123FB's free Basic plan — [123FB](https://www.123formbuilder.com/signup.html). Cognito e-signatures on Pro per the pricing page — [Cognito Pricing](https://www.cognitoforms.com/pricing). Zoho added a native Zoho Sign field in Apr 2026 — [Zoho What's New](https://www.zoho.com/forms/whats-new.html). Formstack gates Sign to Suite — [Formstack Pricing](https://www.formstack.com/pricing).
- **Document generation:** Basic PDF is free or cheap (Cognito standard PDF templates free — [Cognito Document Merging](https://www.cognitoforms.com/support/5/document-merging); Zoho 50 PDFs free — [Zoho Pricing](https://www.zoho.com/forms/pricing.html); 123FB Gold+). **Templated documents are a premium upsell**: Cognito "Custom Document Templates" listed under Enterprise in one pricing config — [Cognito Pricing](https://www.cognitoforms.com/pricing); Formstack Documents only on Suite with 250 deliveries/mo — [Formstack Pricing](https://www.formstack.com/pricing).
- **Enterprise gates are universal:**
  - HIPAA: Jotform Gold/Ent ([Pricing](https://www.jotform.com/pricing/)); Zoho Premium ([Pricing](https://www.zoho.com/forms/pricing.html)); Cognito Enterprise ([Pricing](https://www.cognitoforms.com/pricing)); Formstack add-on or Enterprise ([Pricing](https://www.formstack.com/pricing)); 123FB Enterprise ([123FB](https://www.123formbuilder.com/signup.html)).
  - SSO: Enterprise-only at Jotform, Formstack, Cognito and 123FB (add-on) — same sources.
  - Data residency: Enterprise-only at Jotform ([Pricing](https://www.jotform.com/pricing/)). Jotform's EU (Frankfurt) option is a separate account setting ([Security](https://www.jotform.com/salesforce-forms/security/)).
  - Audit: Cognito "1-year Audit Log History" on Business (Config 2) ([Pricing](https://www.cognitoforms.com/pricing)); Zoho Record Audit Premium-only ([Pricing](https://www.zoho.com/forms/pricing.html)).
- **Tables/ops data:** Only Jotform ships a dedicated spreadsheet-database product (Tables), with tasks and reports, plus Boards (Mar 2025) — [Jotform Enterprise](https://www.jotform.com/enterprise/); [Newsletters 2025](https://www.jotform.com/newsletters/?year=2025). Cognito offers "managed datasets for lookups" and saved entry views — [Cognito Features](https://www.cognitoforms.com/features). Formstack's Streamline instead pushes **write-back to the system of record** — [Intellistack PR](https://www.intellistack.com/press/intellistack-launches-streamline).
- **Offline mobile/kiosk:** Jotform (free Mobile Forms app, offline kiosk) — [Jotform Features](https://www.jotform.com/features/); [Enterprise](https://www.jotform.com/enterprise/). Zoho (offline mode since 2022; kiosk web view Oct 2025; geofencing, geo-stamped uploads, scanner input) — [Zoho What's New](https://www.zoho.com/forms/whats-new.html). Not advertised by Formstack (features page) or Cognito.
- **Partial submissions / drop-off analytics:** Formstack Partial Submissions + Field Bottlenecks — [Formstack Features](https://www.formstack.com/features). No equivalent was found in the other vendors' primary pages in this session.
- **Buyer asks from reviews:** A Cognito reviewer wanted "a dashboard of sorts on forms to see data" and copy/paste of fields between forms — [Capterra](https://capterra.com/p/138990/Cognito-Forms/) [3P]. Another said common workflows such as approvals still "require complex several-step processes" — [Capterra/Software Advice reviews](https://www.softwareadvice.com/forms-automation/cognito-forms-profile/reviews/) [3P].

### Inferences
- For Geiger Forms to be credible to business buyers, the minimum ops bundle is: conditional logic + calculations, repeating sections, save & resume, file uploads, e-signature field, payments, PDF of the submission, email notifications/autoresponders, and a **single-level approval** step. All competitors here offer these at mid-tier or below.
- Multi-level/conditional approvals, Word/PDF templated document generation, a client portal, and a tables/database view are the "operations depth" that separates Jotform, Cognito and Zoho from simple builders (Wufoo, the lower tiers of 123FB).
- HIPAA/BAA, SSO/SCIM, audit logs, data residency and field/form encryption are where every vendor monetizes. If Geiger Forms omits them, it cannot be sold into regulated buyers. Offering some at lower tiers would be a pricing wedge.
- Offline mobile/kiosk is niche (field service, events, healthcare intake) but a hard requirement for those segments. Only two of the six vendors support it.

### Gaps
- No quantitative survey of buyer priorities (e.g., % of buyers requiring approvals or HIPAA) was found. The must-have/niche split above is inferred from tier placement and review comments, not buyer data.
- G2 category "feature importance" data was not retrieved.

## Q4. What do users praise and criticize (complexity, pricing, limits, dated UX)?

### Takeaway
Jotform is praised for breadth and ease, and criticized for price-to-quota ratio, free-tier caps and paid branding removal. Formstack is criticized hardest: high price, weak support (Capterra customer service 3.6), and "outdated/clunky" advanced logic. Cognito is praised for power-per-dollar, frequent updates and support, and criticized for its learning curve on advanced calculations/workflow, limited design control, and multi-step approval setup. Wufoo is seen as dated and stagnant (per a competitor). Independent sentiment on Zoho Forms and 123FormBuilder was thin.

### Cited Findings
- **Jotform:**
  - G2 4.7/5 from 5,326 reviews and Capterra 4.7 from 2,794 — [G2](https://g2.com/products/jotform/reviews?page=4); [Capterra](https://capterra.com/p/158456/JotForm-4-0/reviews/?page=3) [3P].
  - Complaints: "a bit pricey compared to similar offerings", "too expensive for someone who's just starting out", the cost of upgrading for more forms and submissions, a free-tier form cap, wanting a cheaper way to remove branding, and a missing GoHighLevel integration. Jotform's reply: nearly all features are on Free, and the differences are usage limits — [Capterra/G2 via search summary](https://capterra.com/p/158456/JotForm-4-0/reviews/?page=3) [3P].
  - Structural observation: every self-serve tier is single-user; multi-user requires Enterprise — [Jotform Pricing](https://www.jotform.com/pricing/).
- **Formstack:**
  - Capterra 4.0 (121 reviews); customer service 3.6; ease of use 4.0.
  - Quotes: "cost ... can be prohibitive for smaller organizations"; "overpriced, pretty limited. Nothing you can't do with Gravity forms"; "a little clunky, especially when working through more advanced form logic, integrations, or formatting"; complaints about unfair contract/cancellation terms. "The product is outdated. Complex to use" is from a 2024 review [OLDER] — [Capterra](https://www.capterra.com/p/118892/Formstack/reviews/); [Software Advice](https://www.softwareadvice.com/form-builder/formstack-profile/) [3P].
  - The most recent 2026 reviews are reportedly more favorable — same sources.
- **Cognito Forms:**
  - Software Advice 4.7/5 (182 reviews).
  - Praise: ease of use for non-technical users, low cost, frequent new features ("They continue to add new features!", May 2026), responsive support, SharePoint integration, prefill and table fields.
  - Criticism: advanced features "can take a bit of figuring out at first"; limited design/layout control; no bulk send; approvals need "complex several-step processes"; reported lack of subscription payments.
  - Sources: [Software Advice reviews](https://www.softwareadvice.com/forms-automation/cognito-forms-profile/reviews/); [Capterra reviews](https://capterra.com/p/138990/Cognito-Forms/reviews/) [3P].
- **Wufoo:** "used to be great, but it hasn't kept up"; interface "2010s-era"; ratings "increasingly mixed" — quoted by [Paperform](https://paperform.com/form-builders/cognito-forms-vs-wufoo/) [COMPETITOR]. Plan structure confirms per-entry overage ($0.05) and a 10-field free form — [Wufoo Pricing](https://www.wufoo.com/pricing/).
- **Zoho Forms:**
  - HIPAA features sit behind higher tiers.
  - Zoho states it does not handle PHI "for its own purposes" and recommends legal advice, leaving compliance responsibility largely with the customer — [Paubox](https://www.paubox.com/blog/is-zoho-forms-hipaa-compliant) [3P/COMPETITOR]; [Zoho HIPAA guide](https://help.zoho.com/portal/en/kb/forms/user-guide/privacy-features/hipaa-compliance/articles/hipaa-compliance-in-zoho-forms).

### Inferences
- The recurring buyer pain is **quota/seat-based pricing**: Jotform caps submissions and keeps every self-serve tier single-user, Formstack's entry price is high, and Wufoo charges per-entry overages. A suite-bundled Geiger Forms with generous seats and limits could position directly against this.
- **Advanced logic and workflow UX is the complexity hotspot** (Cognito's learning curve, Formstack's "clunky" logic, Cognito's multi-step approvals). Visual rule-flow views (Zoho Jul 2026; Cognito's workflow diagram) and AI-generated workflows (Cognito) are the vendors' responses. Geiger could differentiate with a clear visual workflow/rule editor.
- Support quality and contract terms are a real churn driver for Formstack, which suggests transparent self-serve pricing is itself a differentiator in this segment.

### Gaps
- Quantitative G2 satisfaction subscores (ease of setup, admin, support) per product were not retrieved.
- Recent independent (non-competitor) reviews of Zoho Forms, 123FormBuilder and Wufoo were not found. Wufoo's "stagnation" claim comes only from a competitor.
- Review-based evidence on how well Jotform Workflows, Tables and Sign perform in practice (reliability, depth limits) was not found in this session.
